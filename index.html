Line Tower Wars rules

Rules, towers and creeps are taken from the custom map Line Tower Wars 45 by Loss05, Nob, Trymio and t3tr1s. A fan-made, unofficial game. All art is original.

Twenty-eight levels of creeps march from the demon portal to your castle. Build towers beside the road, upgrade them, and hold the gate.

  • On a touch screen, tap a tower button, then tap a spot twice to build there. Tap a tower for its upgrades; selling it returns 75% of what you spent. Drag to move the map, pinch to zoom, hold a button or a unit for its details.
  • A / S / E build Archer, Sharp or Elemental tower. Hold Shift to keep building.
  • Click a tower to see its upgrades. X sells it for 75% of what you spent.
  • N sends the next level early. P pauses. 1 2 3 set the speed.
